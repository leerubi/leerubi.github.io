Understanding touch in context

How does context shape the meaning of social touch?

Ayush Bhardwaj, Ashish Pratap, Abbas Khawaja, Yapeng Tian, Uison Ju, Dajin Lee, Seungmoon Choi, and Jin Ryong Kim

ACM CHI · Conference paper

PaperWatch video

Methods and focus

  • Social touch
  • Context
  • Qualitative analysis

The question

The same physical contact can take on different meanings depending on the people and situation. This collaborative work examines social touch through its context.

The system and study

The project develops a contextual analysis of social touch. My research internship at the University of Texas at Dallas contributed to the taxonomy development.

What we learned

The work offers a structured perspective on the context of touch, complementing the perceptual and technical questions in haptic interface design.
