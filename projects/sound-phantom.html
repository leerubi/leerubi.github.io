Giving a tactile illusion meaning through sound

How does the meaning of sound change an unfamiliar tactile experience?

Hyunuk Kim, Dajin Lee, and Seungmoon Choi

IEEE WHC · Conference paper

PaperWatch demo

Methods and focus

  • Sound and touch
  • Sensory illusions
  • Multisensory experience

The question

An unfamiliar tactile illusion can be interpreted differently depending on its sensory context. This project studies how different sounds shape a body-penetrating phantom sensation.

The system and study

Coordinated vibrotactile stimulation is paired with several sound types, followed by evaluation of perceived realism, directional clarity, satisfaction, and affect.

What we learned

Penetration-related sounds enhanced perceived realism and satisfaction in the evaluated conditions, illustrating the role of semantic compatibility across modalities.
