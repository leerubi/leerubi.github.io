When contact changes intensity

How does the way a wearable touches the body change its feedback?

Dajin Lee, Gyeore Yun, and Seungmoon Choi

IEEE Transactions on Haptics · Journal article

Paper

Methods and focus

  • Psychophysics
  • Instrumentation
  • Wearable haptics

The question

A wearable's fit affects the force between an actuator and the skin. This work studies how that contact changes the intensity people perceive at the hand, upper arm, and lower back.

The system and study

An actuator and load cell provide controlled stimulation while perceptual magnitude-estimation experiments vary contact force, vibration amplitude, and frequency.

What we learned

Increasing contact force generally increased perceived intensity, with the pattern depending on body location and stimulation parameters. The results inform more consistent haptic rendering.
