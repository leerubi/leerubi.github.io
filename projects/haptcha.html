HAPTCHA

Can touch support eyes-free human verification?

Duhong Kweon, Dajin Lee, Seulbae Kim, and Seungmoon Choi

ACM UIST · Conference paper

PaperWatch demo

Methods and focus

  • Mobile interaction
  • Haptic security
  • User evaluation

The question

HAPTCHA explores smartphone vibration as a challenge channel for human verification. It studies the sensorimotor interaction between receiving a tactile cue and responding to it.

The system and study

Two complementary designs use device tilt for spatial search and touch input for rhythm reproduction. Evaluation considers interaction traces as well as usability while sitting and walking.

What we learned

The study examines both resistance to the evaluated automated attacks and the human effort required to complete the challenges. Security results are specific to the tested threat models.
