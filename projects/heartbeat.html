Feeling the rhythm of the body

How do the location and rhythm of haptic heartbeat feedback matter?

Andreia Valente, Dajin Lee, Seungmoon Choi, Mark Billinghurst, and Augusto Esteves

ACM UIST · Conference paper

PaperWatch presentation

Methods and focus

  • Bodily experience
  • Physiological measures
  • Haptic feedback

The question

Heartbeat-like haptic feedback connects an external stimulus with the experience of bodily rhythms. This collaborative study investigates how its rhythm and placement affect the response.

The system and study

A vibrotactile system presents heartbeat-like stimulation at the chest, wrist, neck, and ankle while measuring heart activity, task performance, and subjective experience.

What we learned

The study highlights how physiological measures and subjective preferences can differ across body placements, informing the design of haptic interoceptive feedback.
