Spatial Tactile Optimizer (STO)

What should a haptic renderer preserve when nearby cues fuse?

Dajin Lee and Seungmoon Choi

ACM CHI 2027 · Under review

Manuscript under review at CHI 2027. An earlier CHI 2026 extended abstract is listed separately.

Video

Methods and focus

  • Perceptual optimization
  • Wearable haptics
  • Multi-target interaction

Research question

Presenting each target independently can cause nearby vibrations to fuse into a phantom sensation. STO treats the number of distinct targets as a design requirement, alongside their directions.

Prototype and study

A model of tactile fusion is incorporated into a mixed-integer optimization problem. A torso-worn array is evaluated in a perceptual study and a first-person game with simultaneous threats.

Findings

In the submitted studies, STO improved target-count identification and supported faster responses with fewer unaddressed threats. This benefit came with a bounded displacement of the rendered directions, making the perceptual trade-off explicit.
