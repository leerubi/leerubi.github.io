Dajin Lee

Ph.D. Candidate · Computer Science and Engineering · POSTECH

I study how sensory feedback shapes what we feel and how we interact.

My research connects perceptual experiments with haptic rendering and interactive systems. I investigate how sensory cues combine, which distinctions people can perceive, and what these insights mean for interface design.

I am advised by Prof. Seungmoon Choi at POSTECH. I am also a Visiting Graduate Researcher with Prof. Oliver Schneider at the University of Waterloo (June–November 2026), working on user intent estimation and haptic rendering for robotic shared control.

Research direction

From perception
to experience.

I use an understanding of perception to decide how an interface should deliver feedback: which cues should form a coherent experience, which need to remain distinguishable, and how that experience changes a person's actions and decisions.

Looking ahead, I am interested in interfaces that work with the body's sensory and motor processes, letting people explore and adjust sensory experiences during natural action.
