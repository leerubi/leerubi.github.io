Dajin Lee, Ph.D. Candidate

Hello! I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Interaction Laboratory at POSTECH, expecting to graduate in February 2027.

I study how people perceive and use physical and multisensory interfaces. My research combines physical prototyping with quantitative and qualitative user studies to inform haptic feedback design, comfort, and usability.

My work spans spatial audio-tactile rendering, wearable haptics, and embodied learning. I also develop evaluation frameworks for multimodal experiences, including PRISM for music previews, to examine user experience, interaction effort, decision quality, and exploration.

I am currently a Visiting Graduate Researcher at the University of Waterloo (June-November 2026), working with Prof. Oliver Schneider on user intent estimation and haptic rendering for shared control in robotic grasping.

I am seeking research positions starting in 2027 in haptics, human-computer interaction, and human factors, including industry research and postdoctoral opportunities. Please get in touch to discuss potential roles or collaborations.
