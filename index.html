Dajin Lee

Haptics · Human–Computer Interaction

I study how people perceive sensory feedback and use these insights to build haptic interfaces.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science and Engineering at POSTECH, advised by Prof. Seungmoon Choi. My research combines perceptual experiments, computational rendering, and the study of interactive experiences.

I am also visiting the University of Waterloo with Prof. Oliver Schneider (June–November 2026), working on user intent estimation and haptic feedback for robotic shared control.

I expect to graduate in February 2027 and am looking for a postdoctoral position starting as soon as possible afterward.

What I want to work on next

In STO, I work on keeping tactile targets distinct. In music previews, I distinguish a richer experience from a better decision. Both ask what a change in sensory feedback actually enables a person to do.

I want to extend this approach to interfaces that work with the body's sensory and motor processes, letting people explore and adjust sensory experiences during natural action.
